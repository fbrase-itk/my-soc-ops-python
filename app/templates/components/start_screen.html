Team Networking Activity

Soc Ops

A quick social bingo format designed for conferences, workshops, and team events.

How to play

  1. 1Find someone who matches a prompt on your board.
  2. 2Select that square after confirming the match.
  3. 3Complete a full row, column, or diagonal to win.

Feature highlights

Easy to start

Launch in one click with no setup steps for participants.

Conversation-first

Prompts encourage short, meaningful introductions across groups.

Facilitator friendly

Built for sessions where quick engagement and pacing matter.

How it works best: run at the start of your event to warm up collaboration.
